# Code of Conduct

Kwentaro adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, and assume good intent. Welcome people of every background and experience level, including first-time contributors and shop owners who aren't developers. Harassment, insults, and personal or political attacks are not tolerated.

To report unacceptable behavior, open a private report through [GitHub's security/contact options](../../security) or email the maintainer at rechceltoledo@gmail.com. Reports are handled confidentially.
